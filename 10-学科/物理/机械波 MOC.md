## 一页公式框架

$$\omega=2\pi f,\qquad k=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda},\qquad u=\lambda f=\frac{\omega}{k}$$

$$y=A\cos(\omega t\mp kx+\varphi_0)$$

$$v_y=\frac{\partial y}{\partial t}$$

$$y_{\text{驻}}=2A\cos kx\cos\omega t$$
